# Welcome to the profile of Dilet!

I'm Dilet, also known by ~some~ most as Ethan. Safe to say, this place is only going to get livelier as I've officially become a CS major.

## More About Me

I made some abortive attempts at game development during my middle school-high school years, and grinded competitive programming hard in high school, eventually clawing my way to USACO Gold in 2024. I took a break from that after hitting Gold to go learn some physics, and I thought that was pretty cool and ended up going to UCLA for physics. But, I still very much am into algorithmic programming - so what better way to combine the two than write some physics simulations?

But more generally, I'm interested in designing elegant and functional systems, which has led to me also being interested in hardware (fixing graphics cards and building an 8-bit computer from logic gates), game development (algorithms and gameplay systems), and even mechanical/automotive work (my engine rebuild project)

Also, you should try out Dev-C++'s ocean theme. Very calming.

## Projects

After a brief hiatus for finals and the summer, I'm polishing my N-body simulator and will have another cool project done or almost done by the end of the quarter. 


### Modern Projects

[SchrodingerSolver](https://github.com/DILET1/SchrodingerSolver), the Schrodinger equation visualizer. Built with numpy and matplotlib. It works, I'm not polishing it.

[PointBalls](https://github.com/DILET1/PointBalls), the N-body simulator. Using the RK4 method and Barnes-Hut simulation. Currently being polished and optimized, and may be ported to C++.

I also have a project car, a 2008 Mazda RX-8 that I rebuilt the engine in, in addition to a space combat game in UE5 I'm beginning to develop. I also serve as a undergraduate researcher in a thermal design lab.
